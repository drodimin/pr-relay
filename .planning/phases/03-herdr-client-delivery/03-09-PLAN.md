From internal/herdr/types.go (AgentInfo; today `LaunchPending bool` and `InteractiveReady bool` are decoded from `launch_pending` and `interactive_ready`). `CompletionSeq *uint64` is nil until the agent completes a turn. `StateChangeSeq uint64`.

Live evidence gathered during planning (2026-11-03, herdr 0.9.5, read-only `herdr agent get w1:p5` and `w2:p3` on long-idle Claude panes): the reply contains NEITHER `launch_pending` NOR `interactive_ready`. The schema marks both as optional booleans. So a plain `bool` decodes "absent" as not ready. Gating on `!InteractiveReady` as a bool would hold every real agent forever. Readiness must be tri-state.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| herdr socket -> worker | agent.get and agent.prompt replies decide whether text is typed and whether subscriptions are cancelled |
| worker -> agent pane | pr-relay text is typed into a terminal |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-03-40 | Denial of Service (data loss) | worker.deliver CodeTimeout branch | mitigate | no ack on timeout; ack only on success or on a completion_seq advance (Task 2) |
| T-03-41 | Tampering (wrong target) | worker.run identity check | mitigate | settled() gate ahead of identify, so a transient relaunch snapshot cannot cancel or redirect (Task 1) |
| T-03-42 | Denial of Service (prompt flood) | repeated unconfirmed re-sends | mitigate | exponential backoff from Recheck, capped at 16m, interruptible by status pokes (Task 2) |
| T-03-43 | Repudiation (false confirmation) | confirmUnconfirmed | accept | a user-started turn that completes while our text was dropped could confirm falsely. This is rare. StateChangeSeq and working-only signals are explicitly excluded |
