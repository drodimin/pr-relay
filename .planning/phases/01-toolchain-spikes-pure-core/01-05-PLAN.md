Line grammar (from RESEARCH "Line grammar (render)", user-approved examples in CONTEXT specifics). P = ref.URL():
```
[pr-relay] o/r#N ci.failed: <check> <check-url | P/checks>
[pr-relay] o/r#N ci.settled: passed (<k> checks) P/checks
[pr-relay] o/r#N ci.settled: failed (<f> of <k>): <name>, <name> P/checks
[pr-relay] o/r#N review approved by alice <review-url | P>
[pr-relay] o/r#N review changes_requested by alice (4 inline comments) <review-url | P>
[pr-relay] o/r#N review commented by bob (1 inline comment) <review-url | P>
[pr-relay] o/r#N comment by bob <comment-url | P>
[pr-relay] o/r#N reply by carol on src/x.go <comment-url | P>
[pr-relay] o/r#N comment by dave on src/y.go <comment-url | P>        (CommentKind "inline")
[pr-relay] o/r#N commits: 3 new, head abc1234 P/commits
[pr-relay] o/r#N commits: force-pushed, head abc1234 (was def5678) https://github.com/o/r/compare/def5678...abc1234
[pr-relay] o/r#N conflict: conflicting P
[pr-relay] o/r#N conflict: cleared P
[pr-relay] o/r#N closed: merged by alice P
[pr-relay] o/r#N closed: closed by bob P
```
